How to play

Paint drops fall toward a row of colored tiles. Tap the left or right side of the screen to slide the tiles, and catch each drop on the tile of the same color. Hold to slide faster. One wrong color ends the run.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock new drops?

New drops unlock one per run when you reach certain scores: Chameleon, Double, Chain, Rainbow, Triple and Alternating. Once unlocked, a drop can show up in every run.

Why is the Daily Drop locked?

Unlock every drop in normal mode and the Daily Drop opens. It's a new challenge every day, the same for everyone, with its own leaderboard.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it back on a new device?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

My score isn't on the leaderboard.

Leaderboards use Game Center. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app, then play a run; your best score is sent automatically.

Can I turn off sound or vibration?

Yes, both have switches in the game's Settings.
