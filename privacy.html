Privacy Policy

How Paint Drop handles your information.

Support Privacy Policy Licenses

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the Paint Drop app ("the app") handles and how. Paint Drop is made by Jordan Overmyer ("we"). If you have questions, contact jordangames.support@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any account details, and we do not run our own servers that collect information about you. Your high scores, unlocked drops, Daily Drop streak and settings are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The free version of Paint Drop shows ads. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with, such as Unity Ads and ironSource. To show and measure ads, these providers may automatically collect information such as:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking, and the identifier for vendors (IDFV)
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • IP address and an approximate location derived from it
  • information about the ads you see and interact with

On iOS, the app asks your permission before allowing apps and websites to track you. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you buy Remove Ads, the app stops showing ads and does not start the ad services.

How these providers use information is described in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Game Center

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. If you're signed in, your scores and Game Center name are sent to Apple and shown on the leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Children

Paint Drop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop ads entirely.
  • Deleting the app removes the progress and settings saved on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
